Genius Typo.

A typing game with no word lists. You get dropped into an awkward everyday situation and have to talk your way through it, live, with someone who may be upset with you.

Your speed and mistakes are tracked while you chat. Every conversation ends well or badly, and which one it is depends on what you say. Go quiet and they will notice.

Your goal:

Pasting is turned off and the other person replies at their own pace. The clock starts when you open the chat.
